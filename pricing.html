Pricing questions.

Why do the packages say “from”?

The starting price depends on a defined scope. Additional pages, features, integrations, or content needs can affect the final quote.

Do I need to provide the website content?

You can provide your own text and images. Content writing or sourcing can be discussed and quoted separately if needed.

Are hosting and domain costs included?

No. Domain registration, hosting, and paid third-party services are generally billed separately by their providers.
