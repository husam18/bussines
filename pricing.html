Pricing questions.

Are these fixed prices?

Yes. Each price covers the features listed for that package. Anything outside the listed scope will be agreed separately before work begins.

Do I need to provide the website content?

You can provide your own text and images. Content writing or sourcing can be discussed and quoted separately if needed.

Are hosting, domain, and Stripe fees included?

No. Domain registration, hosting, Stripe transaction fees, and other paid third-party services are billed separately by their providers.
